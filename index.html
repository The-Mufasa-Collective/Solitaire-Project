How to play solitaire

  1. Match the suits: Clear the board by building from Ace to King on the foundation pile.
  2. Alternate Colors: Stack cards in columns by switching red and black.
  3. Empty Spaces: Only Kings can be placed in empty column spots.
  4. Draw Pile: Tap the deck to get new cards when stuck.
